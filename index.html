About us

Built on 25+ years of experience in the agri industry

AG Enterprises was founded in 2016. Our promoters have more than 25 years of experience in the agri industry, and we are engaged in the procurement, trading and supply of a wide range of agricultural products. Over the years we have built detailed knowledge and expertise in every product we deal in, which has helped us develop an international customer base.

We deal in animal, aqua and poultry feeds such as wheat, wheat flour, yellow maize, gluten meal, rapeseed meal, canola meal, soybean meal and sunflower meal, along with pulses, rice and spices, sourced from across the world.

We follow a strict quality process and control the whole supply chain, from procurement and quality check to loading and shipment. Our large network of suppliers around the world gives us an edge in supplying quality products consistently, with commitment and integrity.

Our spices such as Cassia, Nutmeg, Mace and Black Pepper are supplied as per ISO, HACCP and GMP standards.
